# Presenter runbook: the Stage 1–3 GCC demo

The tested click path for each demo script in `s1-s3-demo-spec.md` §17, as walked in plan 016c (2026-09-27). It says where to switch persona, what the prospect should notice, and what not to click.

The runbook describes; the app decides. Scores, counts and amounts are named by their tile or column, not copied here, because they come from the seed and may change.

**Conventions**
- **Bold** marks a persona switch. Use the persona menu at the top right; it is labelled as a demo control.
- "Demo ›" means the Demo menu beside the company switcher. Everything in it is a presenter control and is labelled so.
- The five companies are Najd (KSA, the primary one), Corniche (UAE), Dafna (Qatar), Batinah (Oman) and Qurain (Kuwait). The hero tender is **T-2026-118**, and it exists in all five.

---

## Before the meeting

1. Open the app in a window of **1440 × 900 or larger**, in the **light** theme (Settings › Theme).
2. **Reset all:** Demo › Reset demo… › Reset all companies.
3. **Prospect branding (optional).** Set it *after* the full reset, because Reset demo clears it.
   - As the Head of Tendering, open Administration › Branding.
   - Pick an accent colour and, if you like, a name to show in the company switcher. Save branding.
   - Presets keep the branding. To undo it, use Restore defaults, or Reset demo.
4. Start in **Najd**, as **Faisal Al-Harbi, Head of Tendering**, on the Dashboard.
5. Keep to in-app navigation during View as (script F): a browser reload ends View as.

## Script A. From portal to Pursue (about 10 min)

**Proves:** a tender published this morning is read, checked against our own credentials, and decided with a reason (M-1, M-2, M-3).
**Company and preset:** Najd › Demo › Start: morning intake.

1. **Faisal Al-Harbi (Head of Tendering).**
   - The Dashboard: tiles, the tender tracker and Needs your action.
   - Tender radar. The hero, captured from Etimad this morning. Its booklet purchase was requested by the Coordinator and approved by a person.
   - *Notice:* nobody searched a portal, and a person approved the spend.
2. **Aisha Al-Qahtani (Tender Coordinator).**
   - Open the hero (search with ⌘K, or from the tracker). Its intake steps show each document read, with pages.
   - Intake queue. Two fields on the hero, including the initial guarantee stated as 1% in one clause and 2% in another. Resolve both with Use ….
   - *Notice:* the agent won't choose between conflicting values. A person decides, and the other value stays on record.
   - Once both are resolved, the hero moves to "1 · Intake · Awaiting DG1", with Omar Siddiqui, its Bid Manager. The DG1 deadline doesn't move.
3. **Faisal Al-Harbi.**
   - The hero › Eligibility & fit. Zakat and GOSI are at risk, because certificates must be valid at bid opening. The "last three financial years" line has two readings.
   - Request renewal on the Zakat line.
4. **Sultan Al-Anazi (Finance).** My requests › Open credentials › Upload renewal.
5. **Faisal Al-Harbi.**
   - The hero's Zakat line now passes, and the "Credentials at risk" tile has dropped by one.
   - The Queries tab: the drafted questions to the employer (VAT; turnover years).
6. DG1 decisions › the hero.
   - Read the evidence pack and the recommendation card ("Recommendation, not a decision").
   - Pick Pursue, confirm the bid team, and write the note as delegate (Omar Siddiqui is the Bid Manager). Review the record › Confirm.
   - *Notice:* the record says who, when, what the pack showed and why. The RFQ clock starts.
   - To record it as the Bid Manager instead, **switch to Omar Siddiqui** first.

**Recovery:** Demo › Start: DG1 due puts the hero at step 6 with its blocking fields resolved.

## Script B. Quotes without chasing (about 10 min)

**Proves:** RFQs go out to screened, approved suppliers, and quotes are compared on a level basis (M-4).
**Company and preset:** Najd › Demo › Start: RFQs out.

1. **Joseph Mathew (Procurement Lead).** Packages & RFQs › the hero.
   - Packages: the scope split into packages.
   - Shortlists: open P-08. A supplier with a sanctions match is greyed out (P-03 has one blocked by an anti-bribery flag). "This is a hard block, not a warning."
   - RFQs: every package issued.
2. Demo › Advance agent work.
   - The scripted supplier replies arrive, declines among them, and the app shows the package board.
   - For nudges and escalations, open **T-2026-104**'s Tracking tab: some replies are overdue and some are escalated. The hero's replies are not due yet.
3. Quote levelling › the hero. Open these quotes in turn; each change shows its source:
   - Tamarisk (P-03): VAT removed;
   - Nordklar (P-03): EUR converted;
   - Rhein Aqua (P-02): ex-works freight and customs duty, plus an exclusion allowance;
   - Hanseong (P-02): USD, CIF, and short validity;
   - Salwa (P-04): a declared deviation and lead time.

   Confirm some adjustments. *Notice:* the agent proposes and the buyer decides, and both are kept.
4. Best fit.
   - On P-05 (fully levelled), Choose another, give the reason, then approve the balanced mix.
   - Do both in one sitting: the override is a draft until the mix is approved.
5. **Ahmed Saleh (Supplier).**
   - The Supplier Portal preview shows only the RFQs Najd sent his firm.
   - Return with "Back to Najd…".
6. **Omar Siddiqui (Bid Manager).** Quote levelling.
   - Levelled totals show; quoted prices say "Masked for your role".
   - Each adjustment is named without its rate or amount.

**Recovery:** Start: RFQs out again. This preset isn't offered in Corniche or Batinah, where the agent recommends discarding the hero.

## Combining scripts

- **A full meeting:** A → B → C in Najd, then F and DG3 in Najd, then D, then E in Batinah.
- **Each preset resets its company first.** B's preset clears A's actions, and C's clears B's. When the company has demo activity, the preset asks first. Branding is kept.
- **Starting a script mid-meeting:** use its preset. For a clean slate in one company, use Demo › Reset demo… › Reset this company; other companies keep their actions.
- **If a persona can't open a preset's screen,** the preset switches to the Head of Tendering and the toast says so.
- **Stage 3 entry and Qurain's DG3** fit after E, in their own companies.

## Known limits (don't click, or say it first)

- Agent timings are simulated on a fixed clock: demo "today" is Sun 8 Mar 2026, 10:00 in each company's time zone.
- Advance agent work stamps supplier replies later on demo day, while the clock stays at 10:00.
- The seeded input times of T-2026-061 and T-2026-042 read before the demo's DG1 Pursue.
- T-2026-041, T-2026-120 and T-2026-072 have no field that blocks DG1: their packs read "Validating" but still take a DG1 decision. Resolve one of their fields first.
- In Batinah the hero is low fit, so DG1 decisions doesn't list it, yet its DG1 pack (Start: DG1 due) counts the DG1 time from logging. Record DG1 from the pack.
- The hero has no nudges or escalations on demo day. Show them on T-2026-104.
- The PDF viewer doesn't highlight Arabic words, and scanned pages can't be highlighted at all (the chip says so).
- The supplier's "Ask a question" in the Supplier Portal is not wired.
- Break-glass access doesn't expire: revoke it in the audit log.
- View as ends on a browser reload.
- Switching company while on another company's tender shows "No … here". Open the hero instead, which exists in all five companies.
- T-2026-119's questions deadline falls inside the expected Eid closure. The screen flags it; it's a talking point, not a mistake.
