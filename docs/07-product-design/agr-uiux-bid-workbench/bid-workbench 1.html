What lives here

Source documents as they arrived, classified bills of quantities, RFQ packs and normalised quote comparisons, decision packs, baseline programmes, priced bills, drafted proposals, compliance matrices, submission receipts, and on award the delivery plan and obligations register. Each artifact carries the tender or project it belongs to, the stage that produced it, and its version history.

What does not live here

Configuration is not an artifact. The capability profile, service catalogue, rate tables, policy playbook, evaluation rubrics and bid templates are settings, and they live in Settings and Templates where they can be edited. This library holds outputs, which are records rather than settings, and outputs are never edited in place once they have been submitted.

How the verdict is decided

The platform recommends bid when the win probability clears the threshold and no hard rule blocks it. Everything else is recommended as no bid, with the reason stated on the tender.

1
Win odds above the thresholdCurrently 55%. Below this, no bid, however good the fit.
2
Eligibility passesA hard fail on turnover, similar work, net worth, registrations or bid capacity forces no bid.
3
Value inside the preferred bandOutside the smallest or largest tender worth bidding, no bid.
4
Capacity availableIf pursuing would exceed the bid capacity ceiling, no bid unless a joint venture is proposed.

The recommendation is never a decision. A Bid Manager can override any verdict, with an optional reason, and both the recommendation and the override are kept for the learning loop.
